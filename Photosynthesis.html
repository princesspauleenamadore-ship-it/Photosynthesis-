Turning Light into Life

PHOTOSYNTHESIS

Nature's Way of Creating Life

What is Photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process where plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to create glucose for food and release oxygen into the air. It is the foundation of life on Earth, converting light energy into chemical energy that feeds almost all living organisms.

Simple Equation:
Carbon Dioxide + Water + Sunlight → Glucose + Oxygen

How Does It Work?

Capturing Energy

Chlorophyll in plant leaves absorbs energy from sunlight, acting like nature’s solar panel.

Collecting Materials

Roots absorb water from the soil, while leaves take in carbon dioxide from the air through tiny pores called stomata.

Creating Food

Using sunlight energy, plants transform water and carbon dioxide into glucose (food) and release oxygen as a by-product.

Why Is It Important?

• Produces oxygen that humans and animals need to survive.

• Creates the primary source of energy for almost all food chains on Earth.

• Removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere, helping regulate the Earth’s temperature.

• Provides raw materials for many products we use daily, including food, wood, and medicines.
